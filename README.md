# Pandemic Simulator

**See how an outbreak spreads, and what slows it.**
Open `index.html` in a browser.

## What it does
Simulates people moving around and passing on an infection. You change the settings and try interventions to flatten the curve.

## How it works
Based on the **SIR model**:
- **S**usceptible: can catch it
- **I**nfected: can pass it on
- **R**ecovered: immune

The key setting is how easily it spreads compared with how fast people recover. Interventions reduce contact or spread.

## Limits (honest)
- Simplified. Real diseases involve age, travel, behaviour and variants.
- Not a forecasting tool.

## Where it fits
Health thread: [LifeKnow](https://github.com/TechTonicCortex/lifeknow) teaches prevention, and this shows why prevention works at population level.

## What I learned
Small changes in the spread rate make huge differences. That's why early action matters.
